Tame Options:

    [Attack Options]

        - "Guard"
            - Attack anything that comes near Owner

        - "Guard Spot"
            - Attack anything that comes near defined spot

        - "Attack my Target"
            - Attack anything I attack

        - "Defend"
            - Attack anything that attacks me

    [Behavior]

        - Passive
            - Don't attack anything

        - Neutral
            - Attack only what attacks you

        - Agressive
            - Attack everything that comes near
